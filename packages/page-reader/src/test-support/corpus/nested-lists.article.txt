A week on the trail asks for less than you think. The list below is the one I check before every trip, in the order I pack it, and the kitchen comes last because it rides on top.
The steps
Lay everything out on the floor first, grouped by where it rides in the pack.
Seeing it all at once is how you notice the second stove you did not need.
Sleeping bag and pad at the bottom
Clothes in a dry bag above them
One spare shirt
Two pairs of socks
Check off the essentials as each one goes in.
Weigh the pack, and take one thing out if it passes a fifth of your weight. The steps above again, if it does.
A loaded pack on a scale
The kitchen
A pot, a spoon, a stove and a lighter. Everything else is a luxury you carry on your back for a week, so choose it the way you would choose a friend for the trip.
