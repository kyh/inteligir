Harbour bridge reopens after repairs

2 November 2025

The harbour bridge reopened to traffic on Sunday morning, two years after engineers closed it to replace the suspension cables that had carried the deck since 1935.

About 40,000 vehicles crossed the bridge each day before it closed. During the repairs, drivers took a 25-minute detour through the industrial estate, and the ferry added four sailings a day.

A slower, quieter crossing

The new deck carries one lane of traffic in each direction instead of two, with a protected cycle lane on the seaward side. The speed limit drops from 50 to 30 km/h.

"It is a different bridge now," said the project's chief engineer. "It was built for trams and horses, and we have rebuilt it for buses and bicycles."

The council will review traffic on the bridge after six months.
