Save links and articles, videos, images, PDFs and files beside the notes you write, in one library that is a folder of plain markdown files on your Mac. It does the work of a bookmark manager like Raindrop and a notes app like Obsidian at once.

Save from Chrome, Arc, Brave or Edge with the browser extension, by pasting or dropping onto the window, or from your iPhone's share sheet; your Mac reads each page itself, without asking a model. When you ask, Claude or ChatGPT, on your own plan, files, tags, summarizes and edits your library with you, and every reply that changed something can be undone.
