# Changelog

What changed in Inteligir (the Mac app, the iPhone app, the browser extension and inteligir.com), newest first. Each release's notes on GitHub are its section here, word for word.

## Unreleased

Inteligir is now a library of everything you save: links, articles, videos, images, PDFs and files, beside the notes you write, kept in your Inteligir account, which your Mac, your iPhone and inteligir.com/library all show. The Mac and the iPhone keep a copy of the whole library, so it opens, reads and searches with no connection. Save from your browser with the new extension, by pasting or dropping onto the window, or from your iPhone's share sheet; the extension or the iPhone that saved a page reads it, and our cloud reads every other link, without asking a model. The agent is built in, the same on every device, with nothing to sign in to, and your own Claude or ChatGPT can reach your library too, through the connector. It does the work of a bookmark manager like Raindrop and a notes app like Obsidian at once.

### Before you update

- **Your library moves into your account.** The Mac app no longer opens a folder on your Mac: it opens the library in your Inteligir account, the one inteligir.com/library and your iPhone show. The first time it opens, sign in, or create an account with an invite code. Nothing on your Mac comes in by itself, and what an earlier version kept in your account's online copy doesn't carry over: to bring your notes in, zip your notes folder and choose Import a zip of notes… in Settings › Import and export. The folder itself stays where it is, and the app no longer reads or changes it.
- **Comments don't carry over to highlights.** An import reads the highlights this version writes and not the comments an earlier one kept in `.inteligir/comments/`, so a comment's words don't come in, and the passage it marked comes in with a dotted underline. To keep a comment's words, copy them into its note before you zip the folder.
- **Your conversations with the agent start over.** The actions an earlier version kept on your Mac don't come into your account. If a reply holds something you want to keep, copy it into a note before you update.
- **The agent needs no plan of your own.** It runs in our cloud, so signing in with Claude or ChatGPT, Settings › Agent and the choice of a default agent are gone, and the connectors you gave the agent don't carry over: it works in your library alone. Your own Claude or ChatGPT keeps its connectors, and can reach your library through ours (below).
- **Download this version from inteligir.com.** The version you have cannot update itself to this one: download Inteligir from inteligir.com and drag it into Applications over the one there. Later updates install from the app again.
- **Inteligir needs macOS 13.5 or later.** The app now runs on the Mac's own web engine instead of carrying a browser of its own, and what it brings in its place needs macOS 13.5. On an older Mac, keep the version you have.
- **Update every Mac and iPhone you use.** An earlier version can no longer reach your account. From now on, a version too old to reach it says so and asks you to update, instead of failing without a reason.
- **Toggles, columns, dates and embeds are written in a new, readable form.** A toggle is `:::toggle` … `:::`, columns are `:::columns` with a `:::column` for each, a date is `@2026-08-22`, and a video, post, PDF or page is `![](its link)`, so each reads plainly in any other notes app. Ones an earlier version saved come in from an import as the tags they were written with around their text, which no longer fold, sit side by side or open a calendar; to get the block back, insert it again from the `/` menu and move the text in.
- **Your window settings start over once.** The theme, the text appearance, the sidebar and panel widths and the spell check switch go back to their defaults the first time this version opens; set them again in Settings.

### New

- **Your library opens in your browser.** Sign in at inteligir.com and go to inteligir.com/library: your whole library is there, to read, edit, highlight, organize, search and undo, and to ask the agent about, with every change reaching your other devices as you make it. It needs a connection; when yours drops, what you type waits and is sent once you're back, and History, the agent, imports and your account say they need a connection until then. What you change keeps going out while the tab is in the background, and closing or reloading the tab asks first while something you changed hasn't reached your library yet. Signing out, or resetting your password, stops every tab that sign-in opened from showing your library's changes, the agent's answers included, at once.
- **The Mac app keeps your library on your Mac, and works offline.** Signed in, it keeps a copy of your whole library on your Mac and opens it from there, so everything you saved opens, reads and searches with no connection, and what you change waits and goes out as soon as you're back; History, the agent, imports and your account say they need a connection until then. A picture, PDF or file you open is kept too, so it opens offline the next time; once they pass about 5 GB, the ones you opened longest ago make room. Your sign-in is kept in your Mac's Keychain, and signing out deletes the Mac's copy. Quitting, or choosing Restart to Update, first saves what you were typing. If your Mac's Keychain won't let Inteligir in when it opens, the window says so: unlock the Keychain or allow Inteligir and choose Try again, or choose Sign out to start over from the sign-in.
- **Your library, one card per item.** The window opens on a grid of everything you saved (links, pictures, PDFs, videos, sounds and notes), with each page's picture on its card and pinned items first. Choose All items, Unsorted, a folder or a tag in the sidebar to narrow it, and a kind (Links, PDFs…) above the grid to see just that kind. Click a card to select it, ⌘-click or ⇧-click to select more, and ⌘A to select everything shown; double-click or press Return to open one, and press Space for a quick look without leaving the grid. Links and files you save land in **Unsorted** until you file them.
- **Save a link from the Add menu or ⌘P.** Add › Save a link…, or a web address typed into ⌘P, saves it into the folder you're looking at, or Unsorted. A message says so, with Open, or that it's already in your library. Add › Add files… adds files the same way.
- **What you save fills itself in, without a model.** A saved link fills in its title, site, author, cover picture and readable text from the page, read by the extension or the iPhone that saved it when it can and by our cloud otherwise, without calling any model. When a page can't be read, the item says why (on its card, point at or tap "Page not read"), and Read the page again, in its card's menu or in Info, tries again. Saving a link you already have adds its tags and highlight to the item you have, rather than making a second one.
- **Save from Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge or Vivaldi, straight to your library.** The Inteligir extension signs in to your account and saves the page you're on to Unsorted, with no app open: click its toolbar button, or right-click a page, a link, an image or a selection. It reads the page as you see it, so the item opens with its text and picture at once, and a selection is saved as a highlight on the page. The first time, its welcome page's Connect your library opens inteligir.com, where you sign in and choose Connect; Disconnect on that page signs this browser out again, and your devices list it as Browser extension. Saved while you're offline, a page waits in the browser, through an update of the extension too, and the popup says Saved when you're back online. Nothing you typed into the page goes with a save: form fields, the choices a page filled in for you and boxes you write in, such as an email you're writing, are left out.
- **Any file up to 100 MB, not only pictures.** Drop a PDF, a video, a sound or any other file on the window and it becomes an item of its own. PDFs, videos and sounds open in the window, and a video or a song can be scrubbed.
- **An item opens full size.** A picture, a PDF, a video or a sound opens in its own viewer, a saved page opens as its article under where it came from with Open original, and a saved link whose page isn't read yet says "Not read yet" until our cloud has read it. Library in an item's top bar, or Escape, takes you back to the grid.
- **Info.** The panel's first tab shows what an item is, where it came from, when you saved it and how many words it holds, and lets you move it, tag it and pin it. Select several items to tag, move, delete or ask the agent about them together.
- **Highlights with notes.** Select text in an item and press ⌘⇧H, or choose Highlight above the selection, to highlight it. A box opens for a note: press Return to save it, or Escape to keep just the highlight. A highlight added from the browser, an import or the agent lands on its passage in the item's text; one whose passage isn't there yet waits at the end of the Highlights tab, and lands once the page is read.
- **Tidy my library.** In the library's header or ⌘P, Tidy my library asks the agent to file and tag what is in Unsorted and everything with no tags; add a line for this one time if you like, then Send. It follows your filing rules, which you write in Settings › Account › Filing rules, starting from a few suggestions the first time.
- **The built-in agent, on every device.** ⌘K opens the agent in the panel's Agent tab. A message you send from an item is about that item, and one from the library about the items you selected, or the whole view when nothing is. Your conversations with it are your account's, so a conversation you start on one device goes on from any other, and it keeps working after you close the window. Each answer's changes are one set in History, and Undo this turn takes them back; before it deletes anything it asks you, a question nobody answers in ten minutes is taken as no, and Stop turns down what it asked and ends the answer. It answers one message at a time in each conversation: one you send meanwhile waits its turn. A day allows a couple of answers, and when that is used up it says when it starts again. When it can't be reached, nothing is charged, and your library works as before. Turn it off in Settings › Account › Built-in agent; your conversations stay readable.
- **Use your library from your own Claude or ChatGPT.** Add `https://inteligir.com/connector` as a connector in claude.ai, Claude Code or ChatGPT, and the page it opens asks whether to let it into your library, signing you in first. Allowed, it can search and read everything you saved, save links, write notes and change your items, and each change it makes shows in History under its name, where you can undo it. Tick Only let it read the library to keep it from changing anything. To stop it, Disconnect it in Settings › Account › Connected assistants, or remove the connector in your assistant.
- **Bring your Raindrop bookmarks in.** Import from Raindrop…, in Settings › Import and export, takes a Raindrop backup's CSV file: every bookmark becomes an item in a folder named after its collection, with its tags, its note, its favourite pin and its highlights. Settings shows how far it has got, with Stop, which keeps what it added, and each page's text and picture fill in over the next while.
- **Export your whole library.** Export, in Settings › Import and export, writes a zip of everything in your library, your notes as markdown files in their folders beside every file you saved, for you to download. Our cloud keeps the zip for seven days.
- **Your library on your iPhone.** The iPhone app opens on your library: everything you saved, newest first, with covers, kinds to filter by, and a search over titles, sites and links. An item's menu opens the original page in Safari and shows its Info; pictures open full size and PDFs open in a viewer, and the PDFs, videos and other files you saved anywhere are there too. It keeps a copy of your whole library on the iPhone, as the Mac app does, so everything opens, reads, searches and edits offline, and what you change there waits under the library until it's sent, then reaches your other devices within seconds. Signing out asks first while changes are waiting; if the iPhone can't remove its copy of your library, it says why and stays signed in.
- **Save from any app on your iPhone.** Share a page, a link or some text from Safari, YouTube, Mail or any app with "Save to Inteligir", or type one into "Save a link or a thought". Your iPhone reads a shared page itself as you share it, so the item opens with its title, picture and text in a few seconds. Shared offline, or a page the iPhone can't read, it's saved as its link at once, its card saying "Waiting to be read", and read online once it reaches your account. A link inside text you share is saved without the quotes, brackets or full stop around it, and the words around it become its note.
- **Highlight on your iPhone.** Highlight in the toolbar above the keyboard highlights the selection; the note is optional. Tap a highlight to add its note, reply or remove it, offline too.
- **Ask the agent on your iPhone.** Ask the agent in the library or on an item opens your account's conversations, the same ones your Mac and the library in your browser show, with the answer written in as it comes and Approve or Cancel on what it asks. Asked from an item, it's about that item. The agent needs a connection, and says so when there is none; to undo an answer, use History on your Mac or in your browser.
- **Photos on your iPhone are pictures of their own.** Add a photo in the library: it becomes a picture item, at most 2048 pixels on its long side and with no location in it, saved offline too.
- **Deleted brings everything back.** Deleting an item also deletes its picture, PDF or file, unless another item still shows it, and restoring the item from Deleted brings them back. Deleted lists files as well as notes, and restores each exactly as it was. Empty Deleted… removes them for good.
- **Terms of Use.** Inteligir now has Terms of Use, at inteligir.com/terms. They say what you agree to when you use the website, the apps, the extension and the connector: your library stays yours, the built-in agent runs on our account within a free allowance, and your library is free up to about 1 GB.

### Changed

- **Updates download by themselves and install when you quit.** When a new version of the Mac app is out, it downloads in the background and installs the next time you quit, so the app opens up to date and nothing restarts while you work. To install it now, choose Restart to Update in the Inteligir menu or in Settings › About.
- **The privacy policy describes your library in your account.** inteligir.com/privacy now says what each of your devices sends our cloud and what it keeps, that the built-in agent runs in our cloud on our account with Anthropic, and what an assistant you connect reads. It follows the sections privacy law asks for, with an index at the top, says how to ask us for a copy of your information, a correction or a deletion, including under U.S. state and European law, and lists every address the apps talk to.
- **Our cloud's request log keeps less of each address.** The log and the traces Cloudflare keeps for us record nothing after the `?` in an address, so a sign-in code or a link's own query never lands there; inteligir.com/privacy says so.
- **History is your account's, and lists changes, not versions of a file.** The panel's History tab lists each set of changes to an item, newest first, by who made it (you on one of your devices, the agent, an assistant you connected, an import) and when. Show this version reads the item as that set left it, and Undo takes the set back while keeping every edit made since. It needs a connection.
- **Your vault is now your library, and everything in it is an item.** A note you write is still a note.
- **Comments are now highlights.** ⌘⇧A no longer comments on the selection: use ⌘⇧H to highlight it. The panel's Comments tab is now Highlights, which lists each highlighted passage with its note (click it to change it), its replies and Remove highlight. Each highlight remembers the words it was made on. On your iPhone too, comments are now highlights with an optional note.
- **The sidebar chooses what the library shows.** It lists your library, Unsorted, your folders, your tags and Deleted, in place of Recent, Files and Deleted, and New folder adds one. Clicking a folder or a tag shows what is in it. The window opens on your library, not on the last note you had open, and Back takes you to what you had open before.
- **The panel's tabs are Info, Highlights, History and Agent.** What Metadata showed, the properties included, is in Info, and your conversations with the agent are in Agent.
- **⌘P searches your library and runs a few commands.** What you type is searched across every item's title, note, saved article and highlights, on your device, offline too, and choosing a match opens the item with its words found; a web address offers Save this link. Its commands are New note, Save a link…, Ask the agent, Tidy my library and Settings. Pin, Move to… and Delete are in a card's menu, and Deleted is in the sidebar.
- **Search looks at an item's address, not its properties.** Searching your library finds an item's address, site, author, description and tags, and no longer finds words inside its properties.
- **Deleting an item on your iPhone moves it to Deleted.** Restore it from Deleted on any device, its highlights with it.
- **A link shows the name its note goes by now.** A link to a note shows that note's name as it is today, the one on its card, so a note renamed on another device reads right in every note that links to it. An item you have open stays open when the agent or another device renames or moves it.
- **Two devices editing one item meet block by block.** Edits to different paragraphs of an item on two devices both land, with no copy made. Where both changed the same lines, the item keeps yours there and says it also changed elsewhere.
- **Callouts in the older form show as written.** A callout from before the five kinds in the `/` menu shows as its plain text, saved exactly as it was, and the links and tags inside it don't count as links and tags. To give it a callout's look again, make it a Note, Tip, Important, Warning or Caution callout from the `/` menu.
- **Embed from URL takes a link on the web.** A link that does not start with https:// or http:// is refused with a message. Add a file from your library as a link instead.
- **The app says which version it is when it talks to your account.** Each request the Mac app, the iPhone app or the extension sends your account names its version, so a version too old is told to update rather than answered wrongly. inteligir.com/privacy lists it.
- **Choosing a new password looks like the rest of the website.** The page a password-reset email opens now matches the sign-in page, and when its link has expired or was already used, it takes you straight to the page that sends a new one.
- **Dragging across the window no longer highlights its buttons and labels.** Only text you would copy selects: your notes, the agent's replies and your messages to it, highlights and their notes, an older version of an item and error messages. To copy something else, such as an item's title on its card, open the item and copy it from there.
- **Long notes and saved articles open, scroll and type faster.** Typing in a note thousands of lines long keeps up with you, scrolling through one no longer stutters, and it opens sooner. Jumping to a far match, highlight or heading lands on it at once rather than gliding there. A task's checkbox reads as "Done" to VoiceOver.
- **Diagrams draw as you type, in the app's own colours.** A Mermaid diagram redraws the moment you change it, in your note's font and the app's colours, and follows light and dark without redrawing. When it can't be drawn, it says why, and which line and column to look at when it can tell, and keeps the last drawing until you fix it. A link or picture written into a diagram is neither followed nor loaded, and a diagram is laid out a little differently than before.
- **An export writes a saved page under `:::article`.** In a zip you export, each saved link's page follows your note between a line of `:::article` and a line of `:::`, and importing the zip reads that part back as the saved page. A `:::article` line you type is saved with a `\` in front, so it stays the text you wrote.
- **A very long page keeps its first part.** When a page you save is longer than a long magazine article, its readable text keeps the opening stretch, cut between whole paragraphs, instead of all of it, so reading it takes seconds rather than minutes.

### Removed

- **A library in a folder on your Mac.** File › Open Vault…, recent vaults, Settings › Vault, the Files list with its Reveal in Finder, Open with default app and Copy path, and the choice of where pasted pictures go are gone: an account holds one library, and folders in the sidebar organize it. To keep a set of items apart, give them a folder of their own.
- **Settings › Agent, Settings › Connectors and Settings › Advanced.** The agent is built in and needs no sign-in; your own Claude or ChatGPT reaches your library through the connector, which Settings › Account lists under Connected assistants. With Settings › Advanced went syncing with a server of your own, Debug logging and Show log: the app keeps no log.
- **Actions.** The Actions tab and its list are gone: the agent's conversations are your account's, in the panel's Agent tab on every device.
- **Let my phone ask this Mac** is gone from Settings › Account: your iPhone asks your account's agent itself.
- **The capture box on iPhone.** The box at the top of the iPhone app's home screen is gone, and nothing is added to `Inbox.md` any more. To jot something down on your phone, type it into Save a link or a thought: it becomes a note in Unsorted. To have the agent do something with it, open it and tap Ask the agent.
- **Raw editing.** No note opens as plain text: every note opens with rich editing, including one an earlier version opened in Raw because the editor could not keep it exactly as written. Importing such a note may write some of it a little differently, keeping its words and lines. A note nested more deeply than the editor can show is left out of the import, and Settings › Import and export counts it among those that couldn't be read.
- **Pictures in a note's text.** A picture you paste or drop into a note isn't added, and the app says so: drop it on the library instead, where it becomes a picture item of its own, and link to it from the note. A picture, video, PDF or other file that a note you import holds in its text doesn't show in the note on any device; your library keeps it, and an export writes it back with the note. Open the one you saved as an item of its own instead.
- **Share with agent, Copy link and Export in a note's ⋯ menu.** Ask the agent with ⌘K instead, which takes the item you have open or the items you selected, and export the whole library from Settings › Import and export.
- **Resolving a comment.** Highlights have no resolved state, and the "Show resolved" list is gone: remove a highlight once it has done its job.
- **Related and unlinked mentions.** The panel's Info tab no longer lists the items that link to this one or the notes that name it without linking, and the Link button beside them is gone. To find the notes that name this one, search for its name, and type `[[` where it is written to make it a link.
- **Problems.** The Problems page in ⌘P is gone, with its lists of links that lead nowhere, missing images, notes nothing links to, and notes that share a name or an `id`. A link to a note that does not exist still shows dashed where you wrote it, and a missing image still shows as missing.
- **Search and replace across the library.** Search across the vault… is gone, with its Replace: ⌘P searches every item instead. To change a word in many notes, ask the agent, and Undo this turn takes the whole edit back; to replace within one note, open it and press ⌥⌘F.
- **Renaming a tag everywhere.** Clicking a tag in the sidebar still shows its items; to rename it across your library, ask the agent.
- **Daily notes and templates.** ⌘D and Daily note in ⌘P no longer open a note for the day, and New note from template…, Insert template… and the Templates group in the `/` menu are gone. Notes from your `notes/daily` and `templates` folders come in from an import as ordinary notes, and `{{date}}`, `{{time}}` or `{{title}}` written in one shows as a formula: replace it with the text you want. To start a note from one you kept, ask the agent to make a new note from it; to date a note, choose Date from the `/` menu.
- **Go to heading.** ⌘⇧O no longer opens a list of the note's headings. Once a note has three headings, click one in the outline beside it to jump there.
- **⌘T no longer moves to the title.** To rename an item, click its title above the text.
- **The Keyboard shortcuts page and What's new.** ⌘P no longer has a page of shortcuts, and Settings › About no longer opens this list; each release's notes are on its release page on GitHub, github.com/kyh/inteligir/releases.
- **Run on an HTML block.** An HTML block always shows its preview, drawn with its scripts off, and its Run button is gone. To see its script work, copy the block's source into an `.html` file and open that in your browser.
- **The `inteligir` command, `npx inteligir serve` with it.** Your library lives in your account: open it in the Mac app, on your iPhone or at inteligir.com/library, and let your own Claude or ChatGPT work in it through the connector.

### Fixed

- **Underlined text stays underlined.** Text you underlined with ⌘U came back as uneditable gray text the next time the note opened; it now opens underlined.
- **Tabs work inside toggles, columns, callouts, quotes and lists, and a tab can end with a list, a table or a callout, or start with a divider.** Tabs anywhere but the top of a note, a tab whose last block was a list, a table or a callout, and a tab whose first block was a divider opened as plain text after a save; they now stay tabs.
- **`<` before a number stays as you typed it.** A line like "under <50ms" was saved as `under \<50ms`; it is now saved as written.
- **A stray brace or tag no longer opens a note in Raw.** A note holding a lone `{`, a tag that is never closed (such as `<br>`), or a closing tag with nothing to close opened in Raw, without rich editing. It now opens with rich editing, and those characters stay exactly as you wrote them. Text in braces, like `{count}`, is now ordinary text you can edit.
- **Notes open on iPhones with iOS 16.4 to 17.3.** On those versions the editor stopped before it could show a note; it now opens on every iPhone the app installs on.
- **Text that reads like a date, a formula or the end of a block stays text.** Text you typed such as `@2026-08-22` or `{{2+2}}`, or a line of just `:::` inside a tab, turned into a date, a formula or the end of the tab the next time the note opened. It now stays the text you wrote, saved with a `\` in front so other apps read it the same way. To insert a date, choose Date from the `/` menu.
- **A highlight keeps a date that follows a web address.** Highlighting text such as `www.example.org 2026-01-02) @2026-01-02` left the highlight reading the date without its `@`; it now reads as you highlighted it.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-09-27

Inteligir 0.6 puts the agent inside the app and your notes on your iPhone. Sign in with your Claude or ChatGPT plan and the agent is ready, with nothing else to install, and Undo takes back what any one of its replies changed. The new iPhone app opens your notes in the same editor, offline, and can ask your Mac's agent. Two devices editing one note no longer stop sync, and the first launch asks where your notes live.

Everything since 0.4.0 (September 4, 2026), and the first Inteligir for iPhone.

### Before you update

- **The agent comes with the app, on your own plan.** Claude and ChatGPT run from inside the app, so neither Claude Code nor Codex has to be on your Mac. Sign in with Claude or ChatGPT when the app first opens, in Settings › Agent, or the first time you press ⌘K; Claude needs a paid plan, and any ChatGPT plan works. A Mac already signed in to either stays signed in. Settings › Agent and `inteligir agents list` show each one's sign-in as Claude or ChatGPT reports it, with the plan and account.
- **Dictation is your Mac's own.** The microphone button in the ⌘K composer, Settings › Voice and the downloaded speech model are gone, and the app no longer asks for the microphone; the model's folder is deleted on the first launch. To dictate, press fn twice (or the 🎤 key), or choose Edit › Start Dictation, wherever you type; the ⌘K composer says so beside its Send button.
- **Connectors moved into your agent's own settings.** Settings › Connectors now shows and changes the connectors Claude or ChatGPT itself keeps, for the agent your new actions use, and signing in to one happens in your browser through that agent's own sign-in. Connectors you added in the app before are not carried over: add them again there (Linear, Notion, Context7 and Exa are one click). Turning a connector off without removing it is gone.
- **Agents ignore agent settings kept inside your vault.** Your vault arrives from your other devices and servers, so settings a vault carries for Claude Code or Codex no longer apply to the agent here; put instructions in the vault's `AGENTS.md` and add connectors in Settings › Connectors.
- **Comments moved into one folder in your vault.** A note's comments now live in `.inteligir/comments/`, in a file named after the note's `id`, so moving or renaming the note anywhere (in Finder, on another device, by an agent) keeps them. Existing `<note>.comments.json` files move there on the first launch. Commenting on a note that has no `id`, or starting an action from it, adds one to the note's properties (its frontmatter). Deleting a note deletes its comments, and restoring the note brings them back.
- **A pin is a property.** Pinning a note writes `pinned: true` into it, so the pin follows the note to your other devices and to the agent.
- **Videos, posts and images from the web are no longer loaded inside a note.** YouTube, tweets, iframes, and images or PDFs from another site show a card with Open in browser. The installed app's security policy already blocked them, so they drew broken; now they say what they are.
- **Two computers adding to `Inbox.md` no longer jam sync.** Captures from your phone are kept from both sides. One side effect: a line you delete from `Inbox.md` on one computer can come back if the other added a capture at the same moment.
- **Signing out frees the device.** Signing a computer out now removes it from your account's devices, so it stops counting toward the limit.
- **A second vault starts fresh.** Every vault besides the first keeps its own account sign-in and default agent, so it starts signed out, with no default agent chosen. Settings says so where it matters. Connectors belong to your agent, so every vault shares them.
- **What your `.gitignore` files leave out, the app leaves out too.** A folder or file a `.gitignore` in the vault names (a code project's `node_modules/` or build output, say) no longer shows in Files, ⌘P, search or links, and changes inside it no longer wake the app. A note you want to see again needs taking out of `.gitignore`.

### New

- **Inteligir for iPhone.** Your notes on your phone, offline, in the same editor as on your Mac, with your conversations with the agent and quick capture to your Inbox. It comes by TestFlight invite and needs an account: sign in with your account's email and password.
- **Edit your notes on your phone.** A note opens in the same editor as on your Mac: type, format from the toolbar above the keyboard, follow links, and ask your Mac's agent about what you selected. Charts, canvases, tabs and other rich blocks show but stay as they are. What you type is kept on the phone at once and reaches your vault when the phone is online. A change your vault could not take waits at the top of the notes list with Retry, Save as new note and Discard, and a note two devices changed says how it was settled, with Open. The note's ⋯ menu shows its comments and deletes it.
- **Make, rename and delete notes on your phone, and add photos.** New note in the phone's notes list starts one, and a long press on a note renames or deletes it. Renaming updates every link to the note; a note another device changed first keeps its link, which still opens the note by its old name. Deleting a note deletes its comments too, and Deleted on your Mac brings both back. The photo button above the keyboard takes a picture or picks one from your library and adds it where you are typing, as a smaller copy without the place it was taken. It all works offline and reaches your Mac once the phone is back online.
- **Comment on your phone.** Select some words in a note and tap Comment above the keyboard to leave a comment on them, and reply to or resolve a comment from the note's Comments. They reach your Mac with the words they were made on, and a comment made on your Mac at the same time is kept beside yours. Like every change on your phone, it works offline.
- **Ask your Mac's agent from your phone.** A Mac signed in to your account runs what you ask the agent on your phone, over the note you asked from, and your phone shows its reply. When that conversation needs your permission, your phone can answer it. If more than one Mac is open, one of them takes each request. To keep a Mac out of it, turn off Let my phone ask this Mac in Settings › Account.
- **Watch the reply arrive on your phone.** While the phone app is open, a reply from your Mac's agent grows on the phone as the agent writes it, and conversations, notes and the agent's questions show up as soon as another device changes them. The phone counts a Mac as ready for your requests only while Let my phone ask this Mac is on there.
- **Signing out on your phone asks first** when it holds changes your vault has not taken or requests your Mac has not picked up, and says how many of each.
- **Your iPhone's backup holds none of your notes.** Notes, conversations and changes not yet sent, photos included, stay out of the phone's iCloud backup: a phone restored from one downloads your notes again. The phone's sign-in stays with that phone, so a new iPhone set up from its backup signs in again, as a device of its own.
- **Undo what the agent changed.** When an action finishes editing your notes, a message says how many it edited, with Undo, and each of the action's replies lists the notes it edited with Undo changes. Undo takes back only that reply's changes and keeps every edit made since, yours included; a note edited in the same place since is left as it is and named, with Open History.
- **Stop a running action.** A Stop button sits in the action's header while the agent works; `inteligir action stop` does the same, and archiving a running action stops it.
- **Create your account in the app.** Sign in… in the sidebar's sync menu and Settings › Account now offer Create an account: your name, email, a password and your invite code, and this computer is signed in to the new account at once, with no second sign-in. Signing in has a Forgot password? link that opens the page which emails you a reset link.
- **Remove a lost device from any Mac.** Settings › Account, which takes the place of the Devices section, shows the account this Mac is signed in to, when this Mac last synced, and your other devices with when each was last seen. Revoke signs one out of your account at once, with no password asked; the Devices page on the web still does the same. When your account signs this Mac out, Settings › Account says so and offers Sign in again.
- **Delete your account from the app.** Settings › Account › Delete account… asks for your password again, says what goes (the online copy of your notes, your synced conversations with the agent, captures waiting for a Mac, and the sign-in on every device) and what stays (your notes on this Mac and their history), and signs this Mac out. If you lost your Mac, sign in on any other and delete from there.
- **More than one vault.** Open another folder as a vault from File › Open Vault…, the vault's name at the top of the sidebar, or Settings › Vault; recent vaults are one click away. `inteligir vault open <folder>` picks the vault the server opens next time it starts.
- **Templates.** Notes in a `templates/` folder are templates: New note from template… and Insert template… in the command palette (⌘P), and a Templates group in the `/` menu. `{{date}}`, `{{time}}` and `{{title}}` are filled in, and `templates/Daily.md` shapes the daily note (⌘D).
- **Search and replace across the vault.** Search across the vault… in ⌘P lists every match with its line, with match-case and whole-word options, and can replace them all. It shows its progress, can be stopped between notes, and leaves alone any note that changed while it ran, naming it. On the command line: `inteligir matches`.
- **Find and replace in a note.** ⌘F opens the find bar under the Find button; ⌥⌘F opens it with Replace.
- **Go to heading** with ⌘⇧O, and **Extract to new note** from the selection toolbar or a block's menu: the selected blocks become a new note and a link to it, and one undo puts them back.
- **Problems.** A Problems page in ⌘P (and `inteligir problems`) lists links that lead nowhere, missing embeds, notes nothing links to, two notes with the same name and two notes sharing one `id`. Picking a row jumps to the link. Two notes share an `id` when one was copied outside the app (Finder's Duplicate, say); picking the copy gives it an `id` of its own and a copy of its comments, so each note keeps every comment it had. On the command line: `inteligir vault new-id`.
- **Unlinked mentions.** Related, in the panel's Metadata tab, now lists notes that mention this one without linking to it, with a Link button that turns the mention into a link. On the command line: `inteligir unlinked`.
- **Tags.** Clicking a `#tag` shows its notes in the sidebar with their count, and from there you can rename the tag across the whole vault, nested tags included. On the command line: `inteligir tag notes` and `inteligir tag rename`.
- **Pin notes** from a right-click in the sidebar, the Metadata tab or ⌘P. Pinned notes sort to the top of Recent.
- **Move notes and folders** by dragging them in Files, or with Move note to folder… in ⌘P. Links into and out of everything that moved are rewritten, whole folders included.
- **Files** sorts by name or by last modified, and a right-click offers Reveal in Finder, Open with default app, Copy path and Copy absolute path.
- **Choose where pasted images go**: the top of the vault, beside the note, or a folder (`assets/` unless you pick another), in Settings › Vault or with `inteligir vault attachments`.
- **Choose your default agent**, Claude or ChatGPT, in Settings › Agent or with `inteligir agents default`. An action keeps the agent it started with.
- **Connect Linear, Notion, Context7 or Exa in one click** in Settings › Connectors, or add another by its address or the command that runs it. One that needs a sign-in opens it in your browser.
- **Spell check** can be turned off in Settings › Editor; outside macOS you can also pick its languages.
- **A Keyboard shortcuts page** in ⌘P, spelled for your keyboard. ⌘, opens Settings, and `[` and `]` hide and show the sidebar and the panel while you are not typing.
- **Facts about a note** in the Metadata tab's About section: where it lives, when it was changed and first created, its words, characters and reading time, and how many notes link to it. The word count and reading time also sit under the note.
- **Link previews you can use.** Hovering a `[[link]]` shows a card you can move into, select text from, and open by its title.
- **What's new** in Settings › About opens this changelog.
- **Diagnostics for "it didn't update".** Turn on Debug logging in Settings › Advanced and restart the app when it asks: it then logs what it did with each file change, each file it indexed, each sync step and each message to an agent. Lines name files and ids, never what a note says, so they are safe to paste into a report, and Show log finds the file. The app keeps what it logs, debug or not, in `logs/server.log` in its data folder, at most 5 MB plus one older file; it never leaves your Mac unless you send it. On the command line, start the server with `INTELIGIR_DEBUG=watcher,knowledge,sync,acp` (any of them) for the same lines.
- **Sync with a server of your own.** Settings › Advanced now asks where the vault syncs: your account, or a sync server of your own by its address, signed in the way your Mac already signs in to it. Your phone and other signed-in devices see only a vault that syncs through your account.

### Changed

- **The first launch asks where your notes live.** Instead of making an `Inteligir` folder in your home folder on its own, the app starts by asking: create a new vault (named and placed where you like), or open a folder of notes you already have. Before you open one, it says how many notes the folder holds, and whether iCloud Drive, Dropbox or another service already syncs it, which that service keeps doing, and it says the same of a place you pick for a new vault. Opening such a folder later with File › Open Vault… asks the same question first. Nothing changes if you already use the app.
- **A new vault's starter notes are about taking notes.** Welcome, Getting Started and Use Cases now walk through writing, linking, comments, the agent and its Undo, History and Deleted notes, and what an account adds on your other Macs and your iPhone, and Use Cases offers five ways to work: meeting notes, research, drafting, decisions and a weekly review. Vaults you already have keep their notes as they are.
- **Editing a note on two devices at once no longer stops sync.** Edits to different parts of a note are combined. Where both devices changed the same lines, the computer that syncs second keeps its own version and saves the other beside it as a new note named after the device it came from, such as “Plan (conflict, Kai’s MacBook).md”, which you can read, fold back in by hand or delete. A note edited on one device and deleted on the other is kept, and comments made on both are all kept. Settings › Advanced lists what sync settled this way since the app started.
- **A folder you open keeps the sync it already has.** A folder that iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive or Obsidian Sync already keeps in sync stays with that service: the app leaves its own sync off for it, even when you are signed in, so two services never fight over one folder, and the sidebar says which one syncs it. A folder that already syncs with a server of its own keeps syncing there and never moves into your account.
- **The sidebar is simpler.** The label at the top of the list switches between Recent, Files and Deleted, with New note beside it; everything else is a right-click. Deleted notes are a list there now, not a dialog, and a right-click restores one. The bottom row carries sync and your account (sign in, sync now, sign out), with Settings and the theme beside it. The vault's name at the top switches vaults.
- **⌘P is the one search.** The sidebar's search box is gone; the search button beside the vault's name opens ⌘P, which finds notes and commands and leads on to vault-wide search, Problems and shortcuts.
- **The top bar shows the folders above the note**; clicking one opens it in Files. Find, Comments and the panel toggle stay in the bar, and Copy link, Export and Share with agent moved under its ⋯ menu.
- **Settings opens over your note** instead of replacing it. The note, its undo history and an open ⌘K composer are still there when you come back.
- **The right panel** has four tabs: Actions, Comments, History and Metadata. A note's properties moved from above the tabs into Metadata, beside Related and Delete note. The panel now starts closed until you open it, and its width is remembered like the sidebar's.
- **Actions** take their title from their first message wherever they start (the command line, the agent, another device) instead of "Untitled action". Notes you @-mention travel with the message and show as chips under it. An action's title, its note, its agent and whether it is archived now reach your other devices. The Actions list loads a page at a time, with Show more.
- **The outline beside a note** appears once the note has three headings.
- **One set of text sizes** across the app's menus, lists and panels. Popups now animate out as well as in, and the app follows your system's reduce-motion setting.
- **A change of more than 90 MB** to your account's cloud vault now says Too large to sync and stops retrying, without uploading it first, instead of failing with a technical error every minute.
- **Your account's cloud vault holds about 1 GB**, counting every earlier version of every note, so deleting notes frees no room. When it is full, the sidebar says Cloud vault full — notes stay on this Mac: your changes keep saving on this Mac but stop reaching your other devices, and your phone keeps its edits waiting.
- **An action another device is running** says so in its header instead of offering a Stop button that could not stop it.
- **Sync reports what it could not send.** Settings › Advanced and `inteligir cloud status` count action events this computer dropped without sending, and a sign-out that could not remove the device from your account says so and points to Settings › Account on another Mac, or the Devices page.
- **The app is smaller.** It no longer carries the command line's source code and tests inside it.
- **The agent talks about your notes in plain language.** It no longer brings up version control or the command line unless you ask, and it never asks you to run a command. Instructions you keep in your vault's `AGENTS.md` still have the last word.
- **Sync and History speak plainly.** The sync row at the bottom of the sidebar says Synced, Not synced yet, Only on this Mac, Offline or Sync paused instead of a technical error message; when sync needs you, Sync details… opens the new Settings › Advanced, which keeps the full detail. Actions sync on their own, so the row no longer offers a separate sync for them. History lists each version by when and who made it (you, the agent, or another device or person), and restoring one says which version came back.
- **The agent writes the same callouts you make from the `/` menu** (Note, Tip, Important, Warning and Caution), and a new vault's starter notes use them too. Callouts already in your notes in the older form still show and save unchanged.
- **One Date row in the `/` menu.** Day inserted the same date chip; `/day` now finds Date.
- **Copy link copies a link for another note.** ⋯ › Copy link now copies the note's `[[Name]]`, the link the `[[` menu writes, instead of a web address that worked only while the app was open.
- **Settings › Advanced keeps the technical detail in one place.** The data folder (with Open data folder), the database version and uptime moved there from About, and this device's id, queue and last error sit there too, beside your vault's sync detail and Sync threads now.
- **Comments you wrote say You** on your Mac as on your phone, beside Agent for the agent's; a comment that names no author shows a dash.

### Fixed

- **An edit that lands while you type is kept.** When an agent or another app writes the open note, the change reaches the screen and the next save keeps it; before, it could stay hidden until you typed again, or be erased by the next save. When two edits truly clash, a message offers Open History.
- **A save that fails says so** and is retried. A note deleted while it had unsaved edits asks whether to discard them or create the note again.
- **Line breaks survive saving.** A line break right after a link, tag or formula no longer joins the two lines, and a note that could lose one opens as raw text instead of being rewritten.
- **Saving no longer mangles** `> [!note]` alerts (saved as `\[!note]`), formulas picked from the `{{` menu (saved with a stray `\{`), an empty inline equation (turned into dollar signs), or `|` inside code, math or properties.
- **Renames and moves keep links right.** A `[[Title|id]]` link follows its note, renaming a note to another note's alias no longer takes over that alias's links, a name containing `#` is written so it still resolves, a moved note keeps its images, and a note that starts with a byte-order mark renames cleanly.
- **Comments** that span paragraphs, or start inside a callout or a table, now highlight and show their marker. A second comment on a note appears at once, and deleting a duplicated note no longer takes its twin's comments.
- **Folding and dragging follow your edits.** A new heading gets its fold arrow, and a fold or a drop lands on the right block.
- **Typing Chinese, Japanese or Korean** no longer submits half-composed text when Enter confirms a candidate.
- **Run on an HTML block** runs the block's scripts, in a sandbox.
- **Agent errors are readable.** "[object Object]" is gone, and an agent that is signed out says so.
- **A stuck or crashed agent no longer wedges an action.** The next message starts cleanly, a crash ends the turn with its reason, a queued message keeps its place in line (after a failed turn too), and a refused approval can be answered again.
- **Signing in again no longer duplicates your own actions**, and the duplicates an earlier sign-in left behind are removed once, on the first launch.
- **Sync.** Saves no longer wait while the network is down; sync the server refuses, or that loses track of where it is, says Sync paused instead of Offline or Synced; a save that could not be recorded shows as the sync error; sync works on a Mac that never set a name for its versions; and one bad entry no longer holds up your phone's captures.
- **Big vaults and big notes.** A very large note no longer freezes the app while it is indexed, a folder with a thousand notes opens at once, a very long paragraph no longer slows every save, and new or deleted notes show up in ⌘P right away.
- **An unreadable file** costs only that file, never the whole file list or the search index.
- **One folder is one vault**, however its path is spelled: `~/Notes` and `~/notes`, or a path through a symlink, no longer open as two vaults with separate sign-ins and history.
- **Quitting during start-up** quits, and a vault switch that fails says so and goes back to the vault you had.
- **Settings.** Choice rows respond to the arrow keys, and a connector says so as soon as its sign-in finishes.
- **A settings file the app cannot read** is reported by name instead of being treated as empty and overwritten.
- **Typing in a long note is quicker**, and the save after you stop typing in one takes a fraction of the time it did.
- **A vault on slow storage** (iCloud Drive files not yet downloaded, a sleeping disk, a network folder) no longer holds up search. A note that takes more than two seconds to read keeps its last search entry and catches up once it arrives.
- **Typing while a note is renamed or moved** is kept; before, keystrokes during the move could be lost.
- **Links in notes.** A note link inside an embed opens the note instead of an empty browser tab, the hover preview of `![[Plan]]` reads `Plan`, and a `www.` address or a bare link in bold or italics no longer makes the note open as raw text.
- **Search in ⌘P's Actions page** finds every action, not only the ones already loaded.
- **Undo right after starting an action** no longer removes the `id` the app added to the note for it.
- **A note an agent deletes** belongs to the action that deleted it, so History and Undo name that action.
- **Your phone** no longer re-reads the whole notes list on every refresh when nothing in the vault changed.
- **A second Mac gets your notes as they are.** Signing in on a Mac whose vault still holds only the starter notes it was created with now brings your account's notes exactly as they are: your own Welcome note rather than the starter one with yours saved beside it as a conflict copy, and no starter note you deleted coming back. A vault you have already written in combines with your account's notes as before.
- **Tab skips the closed right panel.** With the panel closed, Tab and VoiceOver no longer reach the buttons hidden inside it.

### On the command line

- **`inteligir vault write` no longer overwrites a note silently.** It now needs exactly one of `--if-absent` (create only), `--expected-hash <hash>` (replace only if the note still holds what you read; `inteligir vault read --json` prints the hash) or `--overwrite` (replace whatever is there). A script that relied on the old behaviour needs `--overwrite`. It also refuses an empty or interactive stdin: pass `--content ''` when you mean to empty a note.
- **A browser tab signs in only through a one-time link.** Opening the server's address, or a bookmark of it, now shows a signed-out page instead of your notes. `inteligir serve --open` still opens a signed-in tab; for another one, run `inteligir open` or use File › Open in Browser in the app. Each link works once and expires after five minutes. The server also answers only requests addressed to `127.0.0.1` or `localhost`.
- **The command line and the app must be the same release.** An `inteligir` installed from npm refuses to talk to a server of another version (exit code 3) and names both versions, and the app no longer takes over a running `inteligir serve` of another version. Update both together.
- **Flags go after the command.** `inteligir --json vault read notes/a.md` is refused; write `inteligir vault read notes/a.md --json`. Extra words and unknown flags are refused too, where they used to be ignored, and so are extra words after `--`.
- **The model setting is per agent.** `INTELIGIR_AGENT_MODEL` and the `agentModel` key in `config.json` are no longer read, because one model name was handed to both agents. Set `INTELIGIR_CLAUDE_MODEL` or `INTELIGIR_CODEX_MODEL`, or `"agentModels": { "claude": "…", "codex": "…" }` in `config.json`. The server warns at start-up when it finds the old setting.
- **`inteligir connectors` is gone.** Add and remove connectors in Settings › Connectors, or with Claude Code's `claude mcp add --scope user` or Codex's `codex mcp add`, which edit the same list; a script that ran `inteligir connectors add` needs one of those instead.
- Claude Code skips the vault's `.claude` folder, `.mcp.json`, `CLAUDE.md` and `CLAUDE.local.md`, and MCP servers added with `claude mcp add` from inside the vault folder; Codex won't open on a vault that holds a `.codex` folder until you remove it.
- **The app's own git runs skip your vault's git hooks.** A hook in the vault (`pre-commit`, `commit-msg`, `post-commit`, `pre-rebase`, `pre-push` and the rest) could refuse, stall or rewrite every automatic commit and sync. Your own commits and pushes still run them.
- **`vaultRemote` in `config.json` is no longer read.** It applied to every vault the app opened. A vault now syncs with its own repo's `origin`, which it keeps when you sign in: run `inteligir vault remote <url>`, or pin one with `INTELIGIR_VAULT_REMOTE`. The server warns at start-up when it finds the old key.
- `inteligir vault remote` prints where the vault syncs; `inteligir vault remote <url>` makes a git server of your own the vault's `origin`, and `inteligir vault remote account` goes back to your account's hosted vault. `inteligir vault status --json` now carries `externalSync` in every state, and `remoteSource` says `pinned` when `INTELIGIR_VAULT_REMOTE` decides, which refuses a change.
- `inteligir vault open` says when another service syncs the folder or it has a git remote of its own, and `--json` carries both as `externalSync` and `remote`; `inteligir vault status` names the service too.
- **A sync no longer stops in a `conflict` state.** When a rebase meets a note both devices changed, the pass merges instead and pushes. `inteligir vault status` prints a line for each note it settled that way since the server started, and `--json` carries them as `conflicts`, beside this device's name as `device`; the `conflict` state and its `conflict` field are gone. Every commit the app makes names this device as its committer: the name it signed in under, else the Mac's own name.
- `inteligir open` opens another signed-in browser tab; `inteligir action list` pages with `--limit` and `--cursor`, filters with `--doc` and `--running`, and leaves archived actions out unless you pass `--archived`; `inteligir action wait` names an approval it is waiting on, and `--until-input` exits 4 when one arrives; `inteligir action changes` lists the notes each of an action's turns changed, and `inteligir action undo` takes one turn's changes back while keeping every edit made since, naming any note it had to leave as it is; `inteligir agents list` shows which agents are installed and signed in.
- **A browser tab whose sign-in stopped working**, say after the server restarted, shows one signed-out notice instead of an error for every request.
- **`inteligir serve`** refuses to start a second server on the same data folder, and closing its terminal still saves pending changes to the vault.
- **`inteligir open`** against a server started without the app's interface says how to fix it, instead of opening a link that leads nowhere.
- **`inteligir vault remote account`** no longer reports success when the vault will sync nowhere yet.
- **`inteligir action undo`** says so when nothing of a turn could be undone, and points at `--turn`.

### Security

- Content inside a note, such as an HTML block, can no longer reach the app's local server with the app's own access.
- A link in a note opens only when it is an `http` or `https` address.
- The installed app can no longer be started as a plain Node.js interpreter, and its cookies are encrypted on disk.
- The installed app checks its bundle's integrity when it starts and loads its code only from that bundle.
