# Changelog

What changed in Inteligir (the Mac app, the iPhone app, the browser extension and inteligir.com), newest first. Each release's notes on GitHub are its section here, word for word.

## Unreleased

Inteligir is now a library of everything you save: links, articles, videos, images, PDFs and files, beside the notes you write, kept in your Inteligir account, which your Mac, your iPhone and inteligir.com/library all show. Your library lives in your account, so opening, editing and searching it and asking the agent need a connection, and each says so when there is none; your iPhone still shows what you last opened, and a page you save from the browser extension or your iPhone's share sheet with no connection waits there and lands once you're back. Save from your browser with the new extension, by pasting or dropping onto the window, or from your iPhone's share sheet; the extension reads the page you save from it, and our cloud reads every other link, without asking a model. The agent is built in, the same on every device, with nothing to sign in to, and your own Claude or ChatGPT can reach your library too, through the connector. It does the work of a bookmark manager like Raindrop and a notes app like Obsidian at once.

### Before you update

- **Your library moves into your account.** The Mac app no longer opens a folder on your Mac: it opens the library in your Inteligir account, the one inteligir.com/library and your iPhone show. The first time it opens, sign in, or create an account with an invite code. Nothing on your Mac comes in by itself, and what an earlier version kept in your account's online copy doesn't carry over: to bring your notes in, zip your notes folder and choose Import a zip of notes… in Settings › Import. The folder itself stays where it is, and the app no longer reads or changes it.
- **Comments don't carry over.** An import doesn't read the comments an earlier version kept in `.inteligir/comments/`, so a comment's words don't come in, and the passage it marked comes in as ordinary text. To keep a comment's words, copy them into its note before you zip the folder.
- **Your conversations with the agent start over.** The actions an earlier version kept on your Mac don't come into your account. If a reply holds something you want to keep, copy it into a note before you update.
- **The agent needs no plan of your own.** It runs in our cloud, so signing in with Claude or ChatGPT, Settings › Agent and the choice of a default agent are gone, and the connectors you gave the agent don't carry over: it works in your library alone. Your own Claude or ChatGPT keeps its connectors, and can reach your library through ours (below).
- **Your `AGENTS.md` doesn't become your filing rules.** An import lands it as an ordinary note, which the agent doesn't follow, unless it sits at the top of the zip and says exactly what your filing rules say, when it counts as already in your library. To keep what it tells the agent, paste its text into Settings › Account › Edit filing rules….
- **Download this version from inteligir.com.** The version you have cannot update itself to this one: download Inteligir from inteligir.com and drag it into Applications over the one there. Later updates install from the app again.
- **Inteligir needs macOS 13.5 or later.** The app now runs on the Mac's own web engine instead of carrying a browser of its own, and what it brings in its place needs macOS 13.5. On an older Mac, keep the version you have.
- **Update every iPhone you use.** An earlier version can no longer reach your account. From now on, a version too old to reach it says so and asks you to update, instead of failing without a reason. The Mac app shows the library inteligir.com serves, so it's never too old to reach it.
- **Sign in once more on every device.** The sign-in an earlier version kept on your Mac or iPhone doesn't carry over: each opens on the sign-in screen once after this update, so sign in with your email and password, and Settings › Account no longer lists the devices an earlier version signed in. On your iPhone, open the app online before you update, so a page you shared that's still waiting lands first: one still waiting is let go when you sign in again.
- **Toggles, columns, dates and embeds are written in a new, readable form.** A toggle is `:::toggle` … `:::`, columns are `:::columns` with a `:::column` for each, a date is `@2026-08-22`, and a video, post, PDF or page is `![](its link)`, so each reads plainly in any other notes app. Ones an earlier version saved come in from an import as the tags they were written with around their text, which no longer fold, sit side by side or open a calendar; to get the block back, insert it again from the `/` menu and move the text in.
- **Your window settings start over once.** The theme, the text appearance, the sidebar and panel widths and the spell check switch go back to their defaults the first time this version opens; set them again in Settings.

### New

- **Your library opens in your browser.** Choose Open your library, at the top of inteligir.com, and sign in if you need to: your whole library is there, to read, edit, organize, search and undo, and to ask the agent about, with every change reaching your other devices as you make it. It needs a connection. When yours drops, the item you have open says Needs a connection and takes no typing until you're back, and a line you typed just before is sent then; a move, a delete or an Undo you ask for meanwhile waits under the same notice and goes once you're back, as long as the tab stays open. Search, the agent, imports and your account say they need a connection too. What you type is sent as soon as you switch away from the tab, and closing or reloading the tab asks first while the item you have open holds words not yet sent. A tab you leave open stays signed in. If your library can't be reached as a tab opens, the tab says so and offers Try again, and a browser too old to reach your library says so too: update it, or open the library in another browser. Signing out, or resetting your password, stops every tab that sign-in opened from showing your library's changes, the agent's answers included, at once, and a tab whose account is signed out from another device, or whose browser session ends, goes straight to sign in.
- **The Mac app opens your library from inteligir.com.** Its window shows the library you see at inteligir.com/library, signed in the same way: the first time it opens, sign in, or create an account with an invite code. It needs a connection: opened offline, the window says the page couldn't be reached, so choose View › Reload once you're back, and when your connection drops while it's open, it works as your browser does. Close the window and click Inteligir in the Dock to bring it back. Signing out, or resetting your password, signs the window out as it does a browser tab. Give it a moment after typing before you quit, so your last words are saved.
- **Your library, one card per item.** The window opens on a grid of everything you saved (links, pictures, PDFs, videos, sounds and notes), with each page's picture on its card and pinned items first. Choose All items, Unsorted, a folder or a tag in the sidebar to narrow it, and a kind (Links, PDFs…) above the grid to see just that kind. Click a card to select it, ⌘-click or ⇧-click to select more, and ⌘A to select everything shown; double-click or press Return to open one, and press Space for a quick look without leaving the grid. Links and files you save land in **Unsorted** until you file them.
- **Save a link from the Add menu or ⌘P.** Add › Save a link…, or a web address typed into ⌘P, saves it into the folder you're looking at, or Unsorted. A message says so, with Open, or that it's already in your library, and a link that can't be saved says that too. Save a link… keeps what you typed and asks for the whole address, starting with https://. Add › Add files… adds files the same way.
- **What you save fills itself in, without a model.** A saved link fills in its title, site, author, cover picture and readable text from the page, read by the extension that saved it when it can and by our cloud otherwise, without calling any model. When a page can't be read, the item says why (on its card, point at or tap "Page not read"), and Read the page again, in its card's menu or in Info, tries again. Saving a link you already have opens the item you have rather than making a second one, and changes nothing on it, the tags, folder or note you saved it with included: give it those in the item itself. If that item is still waiting to be read, or its page couldn't be read, the page the extension read fills it in. A link you save offline, from the extension or your iPhone's share sheet, is saved the same way once you're back. A short link stays an item of its own under the address you saved, read from the page it leads to.
- **Save from Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge or Vivaldi, straight to your library.** The Inteligir extension saves the page you're on to Unsorted, with no app open: click its toolbar button, or right-click a page, a link or an image. It reads the page as you see it, so the item opens with its text and picture at once. It uses your browser's own sign-in to inteligir.com, with no connect step and no sign-in of its own: signed in there, it saves to your library from the first click, and the popup says who you're signed in as; signed out, its welcome page's Sign in to Inteligir opens inteligir.com for you to sign in. Installing it asks to read and change your data on inteligir.com, which is how it uses that sign-in. To sign it out, sign out of inteligir.com in that browser, or sign that browser out from Settings › Account, which lists the browser rather than the extension. To see whose library it saves to, right-click the toolbar button and choose Options. Saved while you're offline, a page waits in the browser, through an update of the extension too, and the popup says Saved when you're back online; a save your browser quit in the middle of goes out by itself once the browser is back. Saved while you're signed out, a page waits for you to sign in again as the same account, and the popup says Saved once you sign in to Inteligir again. A page it finds no words in, or one too long to save in one go, is saved as its link, and our cloud reads it a moment later. Nothing you typed into the page goes with a save: form fields, the choices a page filled in for you and boxes you write in, such as an email you're writing, are left out.
- **Pictures and files in a note.** Paste or drop a picture, a video, a PDF or any other file into a note's text and it lands there, kept in your library and shown in the note on every device; a picture or video shows as itself, any other file as a card with its name, which Download saves on the Mac and at inteligir.com/library. The pictures and files a note you import holds show in it too. Once one has shown, the iPhone keeps it, so it shows offline there after that, unless the iPhone needs the room back. Pasting or dropping one needs a connection, as typing does. One dropped or pasted just before you leave an item stays in it: the item waits for the file before it closes. A file over 100 MB is refused with a message.
- **Any file up to 100 MB, not only pictures.** Drop a PDF, a video, a sound or any other file on the window and it becomes an item of its own. PDFs, videos and sounds open in the window, and a video or a song can be scrubbed.
- **An item opens full size.** A picture, a PDF, a video or a sound opens in its own viewer, a saved page opens as its article under where it came from with Open original, and a saved link whose page isn't read yet says "Not read yet" until our cloud has read it. What you type under a saved page's text is your note: reading the page again or saving it again leaves it alone, and it sits above the page's text next time you open the item. Library in an item's top bar, or Escape, takes you back to the grid.
- **Info.** The panel's first tab shows what an item is, where it came from, when you saved it and how many words it holds, and lets you move it, tag it and pin it. Select several items to tag, move, delete or ask the agent about them together. If none of them could be changed it says so, with the reason when it has one, and a run that stops part way counts only the items that changed.
- **Tidy my library.** In the library's header or ⌘P, Tidy my library asks the agent to file and tag what is in Unsorted and everything with no tags; add a line for this one time if you like, then Send. It follows your filing rules, which you write in Settings › Account › Filing rules, starting from a few suggestions the first time. A change to them, clearing them included, reaches conversations already under way from their next answer.
- **The built-in agent, on every device.** ⌘K opens the agent in the panel's Agent tab. A message you send from an item is about that item, and one from the library about the items you selected, or the whole view when nothing is, and Ask agent on a selected passage quotes it in the agent's field, ready for your question. Your conversations with it are your account's, so a conversation you start on one device goes on from any other, and it keeps working after you close the window. Each answer's changes are one set, and Undo this turn takes them back for seven days, as Undo this conversation does for all of them, and says when changes made since were kept. Offline, an undo waits under the Needs a connection notice and goes once you're back, and every Undo button stays down until it lands, so one press is enough; if Undo this conversation finds nothing left to take back, it says so and its undo buttons go. Before it moves anything to Deleted or saves a link, it asks you: the card says how many items and names them, or shows the link's whole address. Allow a link only if you asked for it, since saving lets that site see the address. A question nobody answers in ten minutes is taken as no, Cancel on its card turns it down, and Stop ends an answer, turning down what it asked. It answers one message at a time in each conversation: one you send meanwhile waits its turn. A day allows a couple of answers, and when that is used up it says when it starts again. When it can't be reached, nothing is charged, and your library works as before. Turn it off in Settings › Account › Built-in agent; your conversations stay readable.
- **Use your library from your own Claude or ChatGPT.** Add `https://inteligir.com/connector` as a connector in claude.ai, Claude Code or ChatGPT, and the page it opens asks whether to let it into your library, signing you in first. Allowed, it can search and read everything you saved, save links, write notes and change your items; to take a change back, ask it to change it back. Tick Only let it read the library to keep it from changing anything. If you switch accounts while that page is still showing, Allow stops and says so, so the assistant can't reach a library the page didn't name: start connecting again from your assistant. To stop it, Disconnect it in Settings › Account › Connected assistants, which ends its access at once, or remove the connector in your assistant.
- **Bring your Raindrop bookmarks in.** Import from Raindrop…, in Settings › Import, takes a Raindrop backup's CSV file: every bookmark becomes an item in a folder named after its collection, spelled as you wrote it and cut at 255 characters, with its tags, its note and its favourite pin, and each of its highlights quoted in that note, with the highlight's own note under it. A bookmark with an impossible date takes the time of the import, one with a very long cover address uses the page's own picture, and a row too long to read is counted under "couldn't be read". Settings shows how far it has got, with Stop, which keeps what it added, and each page's text and picture fill in over the next while.
- **Bring a zip of notes in.** Import a zip of notes…, in Settings › Import, takes a zip of a folder of notes or of an Obsidian folder: each note becomes an item in its folder, with its pictures and files. Settings shows how far it has got, with Stop. What it can't read is counted under "couldn't be read" and the rest still lands, so check your zip if that count is above zero: a damaged file, a note too long to read or with a list nested more than 128 levels deep, and inside a note a block too large for an item. A link with no address stays as its words, and a picture with none as the text you wrote; either one with a title stays as its source text. A reference-style link or image, like `[design doc][1]`, comes in as an ordinary one, keeping any title it gives, and a definition nothing uses stays as its own line. A footnote comes in as plain words that search finds like the rest: its marker, like `[^1]`, stays where it stood, and its note lands where the file wrote it, as a paragraph starting `[^1]:`. Move the note beside its marker if you want them together. An image, code block, chart or equation that opens a bullet lands as its own block just outside the list; a to-do keeps its checkbox on an empty to-do line above it, so check that line if you tick off items like these. A note copied beside its original comes in too. In a link's file, a page written after its note between a line of `:::article` and a line of `:::` lands as that item's saved page. It passes over what a Mac or a PC leaves in a zip (`.DS_Store`, `._` files, `Thumbs.db`, `desktop.ini`, `__MACOSX`), an Obsidian comment or block reference reads as one, as does a link in a table written `[[Title\|shown]]`, and an Obsidian highlight, `==like this==`, comes in as the text you wrote, equals signs and all. Importing a zip a second time adds no second copy of its attachments, and a note in it with an `id` property brings its item back from Deleted: move it to Deleted again if you didn't want it back. A folder such as your Obsidian folder stays a folder in your library, unless it keeps its files in a folder named assets: then its notes land at the top of your library, so make a folder and move them in if you want one.
- **Your library on your iPhone.** The iPhone app opens on your library, newest first, with covers, kinds to filter by, and a search over everything you saved. An item's menu opens the original page in Safari and shows its Info; pictures open full size and PDFs open in a viewer, and the PDFs, videos and other files you saved anywhere are there too. A picture of more than 50 million pixels shows a plain square on its card, and a page's cover that big isn't kept at all: the item itself still opens, so tap it to see the picture. It keeps what you last opened, so your library and the items you opened still show with no connection, and a picture, a PDF or a card's cover that couldn't be fetched appears by itself once your iPhone is back online. Searching, typing in an item, saving, adding a photo and Move to Deleted need a connection and say so; an item opened offline reads but takes no typing, saying "Needs a connection", until you're back. What you change reaches your other devices within seconds. Signing out asks first while shared pages are still waiting, and erases what the iPhone kept of your library; if it can't, it says why and stays signed in.
- **Create your account on your iPhone.** Choose Create an account on the iPhone app's sign-in screen, enter your name, email, a password and the invite code you were sent, and your library opens.
- **Save from any app on your iPhone.** Share a page, a link or some text from Safari, YouTube, Mail or any app with "Save to Inteligir", or type one into "Save a link or a thought". It's saved at once as its link, its card saying "Waiting to be read", and our cloud reads it: a video or a song keeps its description as its text, and a PDF or picture a site doesn't name as one is kept as that file. A page only your home or office network reaches, or one a site keeps from our cloud, says "Page not read" on its card. Sharing a link your library already holds opens that item and changes nothing on it. A page keeps its whole address, even one ending in ! or a comma. A link inside text you share or type, even one in brackets or a Markdown link, ends where its address does: it's saved without the quotes, the brackets or the full stop around it, and a Japanese or Chinese full stop, a closing bracket it never opened, a quotation mark or an emoji ends it even with no space after. Letters straight after an address stay in it, so leave a space after a link. The words around a link become a new item's note, their line breaks kept, and an unread link is named by its address until it's read, or keeps the title the app shared it with. Words that only look like an address, such as "httpbin.org is neat", save as a note. Shared with no connection, it waits on your iPhone, even if you close the app, the library saying how many saves are waiting, and it's saved once you're back online; one your library can't take waits there too, saying why, with Try again and Let it go. Typing into "Save a link or a thought" needs a connection. A page you share while signed out is saved once you sign in.
- **Ask the agent on your iPhone.** Ask the agent in the library or on an item opens your account's conversations, the same ones your Mac and the library in your browser show, with the answer written in as it comes and Approve or Cancel on what it asks. While it works you see its notes and calls, and a line sums them up when it finishes, such as "Thought it through". Asked from an item, it's about that item. The agent needs a connection, and says so when there is none; to undo an answer, press Undo this turn under it on your Mac or in your browser.
- **Photos on your iPhone are pictures of their own.** Add a photo in the library: it becomes a picture item, at most 2048 pixels on its long side and with no location in it. Adding one needs a connection, and says so when there is none.
- **Deleted brings everything back.** Deleting an item also deletes its picture, PDF or file, unless another item still shows it, and restoring the item from Deleted brings them back. A link you restore, or bring back with Undo, before it was read gets read then; if one stays on Not read yet, choose Read the page again. Deleted lists files as well as notes, and restores each exactly as it was. Delete forever… removes an item for good, and Empty Deleted… removes them all, showing its progress with Stop when there are many; if it stops part way it says how many went, and those are gone for good, so empty Deleted again to finish. Once no item names a file and nothing Undo can still bring back does, it leaves your library and stops counting toward your storage; a file another item still uses stays.
- **Terms of Use.** Inteligir now has Terms of Use, at inteligir.com/terms. They say what you agree to when you use the website, the apps, the extension and the connector: your library stays yours, the built-in agent runs on our account within a free allowance, and your library is free up to about 1 GB.

### Changed

- **The Mac app updates from a notice in its window.** When a new version is out, a notice in the window says so and stays until you act on it: choose Download, then Restart once it's ready. Check for Updates… in the Inteligir menu, or Settings › About, looks again.
- **The privacy policy describes your library in your account.** inteligir.com/privacy now says what each of your devices sends our cloud and what it keeps, that the built-in agent runs in our cloud on our account with Anthropic, and what an assistant you connect reads. It follows the sections privacy law asks for, with an index at the top, says how to ask us for a copy of your information, a correction or a deletion, including under U.S. state and European law, and lists every address the apps talk to. It says what the live connection to your devices carries, your conversation with the built-in agent as it happens as well as the sign that your library changed, and which throttling counters deleting your account can't find. Its tables scroll sideways on their own, so the page fits a phone.
- **Our cloud's request log keeps less of each address.** The log and the traces Cloudflare keeps for us record nothing after the `?` in an address, so a sign-in code or a link's own query never lands there; inteligir.com/privacy says so.
- **Undo takes back what the agent changed, for seven days.** Undo this turn and Undo this conversation take an answer's changes back while keeping every edit made since, for seven days after it. Undo on a link it saved moves it to Deleted, even after its page has been read and it took the page's title, or turned out to be an image or PDF; if you changed the item yourself since, including its description, it stays, and Undo says so. When Undo can't take something back, such as when your library is full or another item now holds the same link, it says so and stays available: free the room or remove the other item, then press Undo again. When everything a change touched was edited again since, it says nothing was undone and stays available too. A change already undone, on another device say, says so and its Undo goes. It needs a connection.
- **Your vault is now your library, and everything in it is an item.** A note you write is still a note.
- **The sidebar chooses what the library shows.** It lists your library, Unsorted, your folders, your tags and Deleted, in place of Recent, Files and Deleted, and New folder adds one. Clicking a folder or a tag shows what is in it. The window opens on your library, not on the last note you had open, and Back takes you to what you had open before.
- **The panel's tabs are Info and Agent.** What Metadata showed, the properties included, is in Info, and your conversations with the agent are in Agent. A property that can't be edited here says so when you point at it.
- **⌘P searches your library and runs a few commands.** What you type is searched across every item's title, note and saved article in your library, which needs a connection, and choosing a match opens the item with its words found; a web address offers Save this link. Its commands are New note, Save a link…, Ask the agent, Tidy my library and Settings. Pin, Move to… and Delete are in a card's menu, and Deleted is in the sidebar.
- **Search looks at an item's address, not its properties.** Searching your library finds an item's address, site, author, description and tags, and no longer finds words inside its properties.
- **Deleting an item on your iPhone moves it to Deleted.** Restore it from Deleted on any device. Search works in Deleted too: open it and type. Moving an item to Deleted on your iPhone needs a connection.
- **A link shows the name its note goes by now.** A link to a note shows that note's name as it is today, the one on its card, so a note renamed on another device reads right in every note that links to it. An item you have open stays open when the agent or another device renames or moves it. A link you write reaches the item with exactly that title, a title with a slash like `[[Q3/Q4 plan]]` included, so `[[Plan.md]]` no longer reaches Plan: write the title as it is. The links a zip brings in are matched to their items as it lands. A link to an item with no title yet reads Untitled and opens that item, an embed of it draws it, and it takes the item's name once the item has one.
- **Two devices editing one item meet block by block.** Edits to different paragraphs of an item on two devices both land, with no copy made. Where both changed the same lines, the item keeps yours there and says it also changed elsewhere. Undo in an open item starts over when a change from another device or the agent lands in it, so it can't change the wrong text.
- **Callouts in the older form show as written.** A callout from before the five kinds in the `/` menu shows as its plain text, saved exactly as it was, and the links and tags inside it don't count as links and tags. To give it a callout's look again, make it a Note, Tip, Important, Warning or Caution callout from the `/` menu.
- **Embed from URL takes a link on the web.** A link that does not start with https:// or http:// is refused with a message. Add a file from your library as a link instead.
- **The app says which version it is when it talks to your account.** Each request the iPhone app or the extension sends your account names its version, so a version too old is told to update rather than answered wrongly. inteligir.com/privacy lists it.
- **Choosing a new password looks like the rest of the website.** The page a password-reset email opens now matches the sign-in page, and when its link has expired or was already used, it says so and links to the page that sends a new one.
- **Dragging across the window no longer highlights its buttons and labels.** Only text you would copy selects: your notes, the agent's replies and your messages to it, and error messages. To copy something else, such as an item's title on its card, open the item and copy it from there.
- **Long notes open, scroll and type faster.** Typing in a note thousands of lines long keeps up with you, scrolling through one no longer stutters, and it opens sooner. Jumping to a far match or heading lands on it at once rather than gliding there. A task's checkbox reads as "Done" to VoiceOver.
- **Diagrams draw as you type, in the app's own colours.** A Mermaid diagram redraws the moment you change it, in your note's font and the app's colours, and follows light and dark without redrawing. When it can't be drawn, it says why, and which line and column to look at when it can tell, and keeps the last drawing until you fix it. A link or picture written into a diagram is neither followed nor loaded, and a diagram is laid out a little differently than before.
- **A very long page keeps its first part.** When a page you save is longer than a long magazine article, its readable text keeps the opening stretch, cut between whole paragraphs, so reading it takes seconds.
- **Signing in on inteligir.com takes you to your library.** Signing in or creating an account on the site now lands in your library instead of on Devices, unless a page sent you there to sign in, in which case you go back to that page, the extension's Connect page and a connector's consent page included. Forgot your password? keeps that place too: sign in from the reset page, even in another browser.
- **Settings › Account lists every device signed in to your account.** Each browser, Mac and iPhone signed in is a row named for it, such as Chrome on Mac or iPhone, with This device marked. Sign out beside any other signs it out at once, with no password asked, which is how you sign out a lost phone. Sign out at the bottom signs out only the browser or Mac you're on, and your other devices stay signed in.
- **A sign-in lasts 60 days from the last time you used it.** Each day you use a device pushes that back, on the website, the Mac and the iPhone alike, so a device you haven't opened in 60 days asks you to sign in again. The iPhone used to stay signed in for good, and the website for seven days.
- **You can be signed in on up to 20 devices and browsers at once.** Signing in on one more signs out the one you used longest ago.
- **Resetting your password signs you out everywhere**, your iPhone and the browser extension included: sign in again on each with the new password.
- **Your iPhone asks for no device name.** Signing in on the iPhone takes only your email and password, and Settings › Account lists it as iPhone. Signing it out from there signs the phone out the next time it opens or reaches your library.
- **inteligir.com describes the library in your account.** The home page, About and the summary for assistants now say your library lives in your account and on your devices, an account is required with its first 1 GB free, the agent is built in, files go up to 100 MB, and our cloud reads a page your device can't.
- **A full library takes nothing new.** Past about 1 GB, counting what Undo keeps of the last seven days' changes, your library refuses new words in a note or in any field, such as a description, a tag or a property, new files and new empty items, and says it is full. Deleting, moving and pinning still work. An item in Deleted still takes up room, so use Delete forever… or Empty Deleted… to free some.
- **A change your library can't take says why.** A folder named like one beside it is put back as it was, and a message says why: rename the other folder and try again. An item you restore while another item holds its address stays in Deleted, and a message says so: delete the other item, then restore it again. On your iPhone, a page or text you shared that your library can't take waits above the library, saying why, with Try again and Let it go, and the rest of what you shared keeps going.

### Fixed

- **Underlined text stays underlined.** Text you underlined with ⌘U came back as uneditable gray text the next time the note opened; it now opens underlined.
- **Tabs work inside toggles, columns, callouts, quotes and lists, and a tab can end with a list, a table or a callout, or start with a divider.** Tabs anywhere but the top of a note, a tab whose last block was a list, a table or a callout, and a tab whose first block was a divider opened as plain text after a save; they now stay tabs. So does a tab whose label starts with a date or a symbol. To keep such text a label when you write it by hand, put a backslash before it: `=== \@2026-08-22`, and for a web address or an email `=== https\://example.com`.
- **`<` before a number stays as you typed it.** A line like "under <50ms" was saved as `under \<50ms`; it is now saved as written.
- **A stray brace or tag no longer opens a note in Raw.** A note holding a lone `{`, a tag that is never closed (such as `<br>`), or a closing tag with nothing to close opened in Raw, without rich editing. It now opens with rich editing, and those characters stay exactly as you wrote them. Text in braces, like `{count}`, is now ordinary text you can edit.
- **Notes open on iPhones with iOS 16.4 to 17.3.** On those versions the editor stopped before it could show a note; it now opens on every iPhone the app installs on.
- **Text that reads like a formula or the end of a block stays text.** Text you typed such as `{{2+2}}`, or a line of just `:::` inside a tab, turned into a formula or the end of the tab the next time the note opened. It now stays the text you wrote, saved with a `\` in front so other apps read it the same way. Text you type such as `@2026-08-22` stays text too: to insert a date, choose Date from the `/` menu. So does text that looks like a comment or a block reference (`%%x%%`, a line ending in ` ^2`), saved the same way.
- **An embed of one heading or one block shows only that part.** `![[Plans#Rollback]]` drew the whole note; it now draws that heading and what stands under it. If that heading or block is gone, the embed says so: fix the link or embed the whole item.
- **Typing `md` in the `[[` picker no longer lists every note.** It now matches an item's title alone.
- **An equation keeps the text on its `$$` line.** `$$ E = mc^2` keeps that text as the expression's first line.
- **An empty bullet keeps the list nested under it.** Both come through an import and an agent's edit, and a bullet that opens straight on another list, like `- - a`, reads the same way.
- **A table cell keeps its second line and its bars.** A `<br/>` in a cell reads as the cell's next line, so a cell's two lines stay two through an import and an agent's edit, and an image in a cell stands on its own line. `<br>` stays a tag: write `<br/>` for a new line in a cell. A code span with a backslash right before a `|` no longer splits its row: the backslash doubles when the agent edits the table, so `a\|b` comes back as `a\\|b`; drop the extra one if you need the exact text. `\|` inside an equation in a table reads as a single bar, as it does in any table: write `\Vert` for a double bar.
- **A link keeps its address through an import and an agent's edit.** A link to an address that ends in punctuation, such as `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma!`, or with a word straight after it, comes back whole. The agent sees it written with its label, as `[address](address)`.
- **Text inside an HTML tag keeps its backslashes.** `\*` in a tag stays a star through an import and an agent's edit instead of turning the words around it italic.

- **Comments you wrote say You** on your Mac as on your phone, beside Agent for the agent's; a comment that names no author shows a dash.

### Fixed

- **An edit that lands while you type is kept.** When an agent or another app writes the open note, the change reaches the screen and the next save keeps it; before, it could stay hidden until you typed again, or be erased by the next save. When two edits truly clash, a message offers Open History.
- **A save that fails says so** and is retried. A note deleted while it had unsaved edits asks whether to discard them or create the note again.
- **Line breaks survive saving.** A line break right after a link, tag or formula no longer joins the two lines, and a note that could lose one opens as raw text instead of being rewritten.
- **Saving no longer mangles** `> [!note]` alerts (saved as `\[!note]`), formulas picked from the `{{` menu (saved with a stray `\{`), an empty inline equation (turned into dollar signs), or `|` inside code, math or properties.
- **Renames and moves keep links right.** A `[[Title|id]]` link follows its note, renaming a note to another note's alias no longer takes over that alias's links, a name containing `#` is written so it still resolves, a moved note keeps its images, and a note that starts with a byte-order mark renames cleanly.
- **Comments** that span paragraphs, or start inside a callout or a table, now highlight and show their marker. A second comment on a note appears at once, and deleting a duplicated note no longer takes its twin's comments.
- **Folding and dragging follow your edits.** A new heading gets its fold arrow, and a fold or a drop lands on the right block.
- **Typing Chinese, Japanese or Korean** no longer submits half-composed text when Enter confirms a candidate.
- **Run on an HTML block** runs the block's scripts, in a sandbox.
- **Agent errors are readable.** "[object Object]" is gone, and an agent that is signed out says so.
- **A stuck or crashed agent no longer wedges an action.** The next message starts cleanly, a crash ends the turn with its reason, a queued message keeps its place in line (after a failed turn too), and a refused approval can be answered again.
- **Signing in again no longer duplicates your own actions**, and the duplicates an earlier sign-in left behind are removed once, on the first launch.
- **Sync.** Saves no longer wait while the network is down; sync the server refuses, or that loses track of where it is, says Sync paused instead of Offline or Synced; a save that could not be recorded shows as the sync error; sync works on a Mac that never set a name for its versions; and one bad entry no longer holds up your phone's captures.
- **Big vaults and big notes.** A very large note no longer freezes the app while it is indexed, a folder with a thousand notes opens at once, a very long paragraph no longer slows every save, and new or deleted notes show up in ⌘P right away.
- **An unreadable file** costs only that file, never the whole file list or the search index.
- **One folder is one vault**, however its path is spelled: `~/Notes` and `~/notes`, or a path through a symlink, no longer open as two vaults with separate sign-ins and history.
- **Quitting during start-up** quits, and a vault switch that fails says so and goes back to the vault you had.
- **Settings.** Choice rows respond to the arrow keys, and a connector says so as soon as its sign-in finishes.
- **A settings file the app cannot read** is reported by name instead of being treated as empty and overwritten.
- **Typing in a long note is quicker**, and the save after you stop typing in one takes a fraction of the time it did.
- **A vault on slow storage** (iCloud Drive files not yet downloaded, a sleeping disk, a network folder) no longer holds up search. A note that takes more than two seconds to read keeps its last search entry and catches up once it arrives.
- **Typing while a note is renamed or moved** is kept; before, keystrokes during the move could be lost.
- **Links in notes.** A note link inside an embed opens the note instead of an empty browser tab, the hover preview of `![[Plan]]` reads `Plan`, and a `www.` address or a bare link in bold or italics no longer makes the note open as raw text.
- **Search in ⌘P's Actions page** finds every action, not only the ones already loaded.
- **Undo right after starting an action** no longer removes the `id` the app added to the note for it.
- **A note an agent deletes** belongs to the action that deleted it, so History and Undo name that action.
- **Your phone** no longer re-reads the whole notes list on every refresh when nothing in the vault changed.
- **A second Mac gets your notes as they are.** Signing in on a Mac whose vault still holds only the starter notes it was created with now brings your account's notes exactly as they are: your own Welcome note rather than the starter one with yours saved beside it as a conflict copy, and no starter note you deleted coming back. A vault you have already written in combines with your account's notes as before.
- **Tab skips the closed right panel.** With the panel closed, Tab and VoiceOver no longer reach the buttons hidden inside it.

### On the command line

- **`inteligir vault write` no longer overwrites a note silently.** It now needs exactly one of `--if-absent` (create only), `--expected-hash <hash>` (replace only if the note still holds what you read; `inteligir vault read --json` prints the hash) or `--overwrite` (replace whatever is there). A script that relied on the old behaviour needs `--overwrite`. It also refuses an empty or interactive stdin: pass `--content ''` when you mean to empty a note.
- **A browser tab signs in only through a one-time link.** Opening the server's address, or a bookmark of it, now shows a signed-out page instead of your notes. `inteligir serve --open` still opens a signed-in tab; for another one, run `inteligir open` or use File › Open in Browser in the app. Each link works once and expires after five minutes. The server also answers only requests addressed to `127.0.0.1` or `localhost`.
- **The command line and the app must be the same release.** An `inteligir` installed from npm refuses to talk to a server of another version (exit code 3) and names both versions, and the app no longer takes over a running `inteligir serve` of another version. Update both together.
- **Flags go after the command.** `inteligir --json vault read notes/a.md` is refused; write `inteligir vault read notes/a.md --json`. Extra words and unknown flags are refused too, where they used to be ignored, and so are extra words after `--`.
- **The model setting is per agent.** `INTELIGIR_AGENT_MODEL` and the `agentModel` key in `config.json` are no longer read, because one model name was handed to both agents. Set `INTELIGIR_CLAUDE_MODEL` or `INTELIGIR_CODEX_MODEL`, or `"agentModels": { "claude": "…", "codex": "…" }` in `config.json`. The server warns at start-up when it finds the old setting.
- **`inteligir connectors` is gone.** Add and remove connectors in Settings › Connectors, or with Claude Code's `claude mcp add --scope user` or Codex's `codex mcp add`, which edit the same list; a script that ran `inteligir connectors add` needs one of those instead.
- Claude Code skips the vault's `.claude` folder, `.mcp.json`, `CLAUDE.md` and `CLAUDE.local.md`, and MCP servers added with `claude mcp add` from inside the vault folder; Codex won't open on a vault that holds a `.codex` folder until you remove it.
- **The app's own git runs skip your vault's git hooks.** A hook in the vault (`pre-commit`, `commit-msg`, `post-commit`, `pre-rebase`, `pre-push` and the rest) could refuse, stall or rewrite every automatic commit and sync. Your own commits and pushes still run them.
- **`vaultRemote` in `config.json` is no longer read.** It applied to every vault the app opened. A vault now syncs with its own repo's `origin`, which it keeps when you sign in: run `inteligir vault remote <url>`, or pin one with `INTELIGIR_VAULT_REMOTE`. The server warns at start-up when it finds the old key.
- `inteligir vault remote` prints where the vault syncs; `inteligir vault remote <url>` makes a git server of your own the vault's `origin`, and `inteligir vault remote account` goes back to your account's hosted vault. `inteligir vault status --json` now carries `externalSync` in every state, and `remoteSource` says `pinned` when `INTELIGIR_VAULT_REMOTE` decides, which refuses a change.
- `inteligir vault open` says when another service syncs the folder or it has a git remote of its own, and `--json` carries both as `externalSync` and `remote`; `inteligir vault status` names the service too.
- **A sync no longer stops in a `conflict` state.** When a rebase meets a note both devices changed, the pass merges instead and pushes. `inteligir vault status` prints a line for each note it settled that way since the server started, and `--json` carries them as `conflicts`, beside this device's name as `device`; the `conflict` state and its `conflict` field are gone. Every commit the app makes names this device as its committer: the name it signed in under, else the Mac's own name.
- `inteligir open` opens another signed-in browser tab; `inteligir action list` pages with `--limit` and `--cursor`, filters with `--doc` and `--running`, and leaves archived actions out unless you pass `--archived`; `inteligir action wait` names an approval it is waiting on, and `--until-input` exits 4 when one arrives; `inteligir action changes` lists the notes each of an action's turns changed, and `inteligir action undo` takes one turn's changes back while keeping every edit made since, naming any note it had to leave as it is; `inteligir agents list` shows which agents are installed and signed in.
- **A browser tab whose sign-in stopped working**, say after the server restarted, shows one signed-out notice instead of an error for every request.
- **`inteligir serve`** refuses to start a second server on the same data folder, and closing its terminal still saves pending changes to the vault.
- **`inteligir open`** against a server started without the app's interface says how to fix it, instead of opening a link that leads nowhere.
- **`inteligir vault remote account`** no longer reports success when the vault will sync nowhere yet.
- **`inteligir action undo`** says so when nothing of a turn could be undone, and points at `--turn`.

### Security

- Content inside a note, such as an HTML block, can no longer reach the app's local server with the app's own access.
- A link in a note opens only when it is an `http` or `https` address.
- The installed app can no longer be started as a plain Node.js interpreter, and its cookies are encrypted on disk.
- The installed app checks its bundle's integrity when it starts and loads its code only from that bundle.
